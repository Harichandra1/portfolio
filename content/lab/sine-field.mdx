The lab is for things that don't deserve a project page. Rough is fine here.

```glsl
float wave =
  sin(p.x * 0.55 + uTime * 0.62) *
  cos(p.z * 0.48 + uTime * 0.41);
```

Multiplying two waves at unrelated frequencies gives a period long enough that
the eye never catches the loop. Adding them instead produces obvious banding.
