## Structure worth copying

Lead with the problem, not the stack. Somebody skimming decides in about four
seconds whether to keep reading.

1. **Problem** — what was broken or missing.
2. **Approach** — the one or two decisions that actually mattered.
3. **Outcome** — a number if you have one.

<Callout>
  Frontmatter is validated at build time. Misspell a key or drop `summary` and `pnpm
  build` fails with the file name and the offending field.
</Callout>
