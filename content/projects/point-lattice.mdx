with actual work when you have some.

## What it does

Every point is positioned once on the CPU into a `BufferGeometry`, then
displaced entirely in the vertex shader. Nothing is uploaded per frame except a
single time uniform, which is why 4,096 points cost effectively nothing.

## The interesting part

Point sprites render as squares. Turning one into a soft disc is two lines in
the fragment shader:

```glsl title="hero/shader.ts"
float d = length(gl_PointCoord - 0.5);
float alpha = smoothstep(0.5, 0.12, d) * vAlpha;
```

<Callout type="tip">
  `discard` on near-zero alpha matters here — without it, thousands of fully transparent
  fragments still pay the blending cost.
</Callout>
